Delete your data

Diet Diary · Updated 15 October 2026

Developer and operator: Sergey Efremov (Efreitor2001), Russia. Contact: pokimoha.123@gmail.com. Android package: ru.dietdiary.offline.

These instructions are available without signing in or reinstalling. There is no separate Diet Diary account; you do not need to delete your Google account. Your diary is stored on your devices, in optional snapshots in your Google Drive, and in files you exported yourself.

How to delete all diary copies

  1. Stop synchronization on every device. Open “More” → “Cloud synchronization” → “Disconnect account”. This deletes the local Google session but keeps the diary and cloud snapshots. If you cannot access the app, first revoke its permission in Google Account connections.
  2. Delete cloud snapshots. If you connected Drive, open Google Drive settings for that same account. Under “Manage apps”, find “Diet Diary”, “Дневник диеты”, “饮食日记” or the name Google showed at sign-in. Select “Options” → “Delete hidden app data”. If the mobile page does not show this section, use desktop site mode or a computer.
  3. Revoke Google permission. In Google Account connections, select this app and remove its access if you have not done so. Revocation alone does not erase existing snapshots.
  4. Clear every device. In Android settings, open the app's information → storage → clear data/storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. Repeat on every device where the diary is installed.
  5. Delete separately saved files. Find exported JSON backups in the folders and cloud storage you selected, including copies you sent elsewhere. Clearing the app and hidden Drive data does not affect those files; deletion by another recipient is controlled by that recipient.

If you never connected Google Drive, skip the Drive and Google-permission steps. Clear your devices and separately saved backups. Reconnecting a device that still contains records may upload them again.

What is deleted and what may remain

ActionResult
Delete a diary recordThe record disappears from the interface; a deletion marker is synchronized. Earlier diary-field values may remain in metadata, older snapshots and exports.
Turn off automatic synchronizationStops automatic exchanges; the connection, records and cloud copies remain. Manual synchronization is still available.
Disconnect Google in the appDeletes the local session and connection cache and stops synchronization on this device. Does not revoke the Google permission itself or delete the diary, snapshots or Google account.
Clear Android storage / uninstallDeletes local records, settings, metadata, internal backups and the saved Google session on this device. Does not delete Drive copies or exported files.
Delete hidden Google Drive dataDeletes the app's cloud data in the selected account; local and exported copies remain.

There is no automatic period for complete diary deletion. After successful synchronization, the app attempts to retain the new snapshot and one previous snapshot from its own installation; other installations' snapshots and copies that could not be pruned may remain. Automatic pruning therefore does not replace the steps above.

Deleting the diary does not automatically delete technical advertising-service data, third-party service history or support correspondence. See the SDK and service list for those recipients and their rules.

Help and deletion requests

Email pokimoha.123@gmail.com with the app name and the step causing difficulty. You can also request deletion of information previously sent to support or ask about access, correction and restriction of processing.

The developer has no administrative access to your devices or personal Google Drive and cannot erase those copies for you. Do not send a password, tokens or a complete diary JSON backup. Minimal additional information may be needed to verify and answer your request. Do not post sensitive information in public GitHub Issues.

If a third-party page is unavailable from your network, its operator controls access to it; contact diary support for help with available actions. This does not give the developer the ability to remotely erase storage controlled by others.
