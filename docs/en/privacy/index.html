1. Diary on your device

The main features work without registration or internet access. There is no separate Diet Diary account. The app stores the following in its private storage on your device: foods, nutritional values and food notes; portions, meal names and dates; weight, waist measurements, body fat percentage, steps, sleep, workout descriptions, notes, manually entered daily calories and protein; goals, achievements, settings and change metadata.

This information is used to calculate calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates and provide history, charts, goals and achievements. You may leave optional fields empty. Steps, sleep and measurements are entered manually: the app does not read Health Connect, activity sensors or medical devices.

Sensitive health information. Food records, weight, waist size, body-fat percentage, sleep, activity and wellbeing notes you enter may reveal health information. The app processes them on your device for calculations, history, charts and goals. If you choose to connect Google Drive, this information also forms part of complete diary snapshots for backup and synchronization between your devices; exports include it in the JSON file you choose. This information is not passed as advertising parameters.

You can leave optional fields empty and use the app without connecting Google Drive. Avoid unnecessary details in free-text notes. Your diary and health information are not sent to the developer's payment server. If you do not connect Google Drive or share a backup, your records remain on your device.

2. Optional Google Drive connection

At your request, the app opens Google sign-in in the system browser. It requests these OAuth permissions:

openid
The selected Google account's unique identifier links synchronization state to the correct account when signing in or switching accounts.
email
The email address and verification status are used to check and display the connected account.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata
Creates, lists, reads and deletes the app's own snapshots in your Google Drive's hidden appDataFolder. Access to ordinary Drive documents and photos is not requested.

The app does not request access to Gmail messages, contacts or your calendar. You enter your Google password on Google's side; it is not provided to the app.

Authorization data. The app receives your email, account identifier, email verification status, and access and refresh tokens. They support account connection, account display and authorized exchanges without repeated sign-in. The local session is encrypted; the synchronization cache, including the account identifier, is kept in private app preferences. This information is not uploaded to a developer server or included in diary JSON backups.

What goes into the cloud. Snapshots contain foods and nutritional values, portions and meal dates, measurements and other entered indicators, notes, workouts, goals, achievements, transferable settings and merge metadata: random installation identifiers, timestamps and deletion markers. The app reads its own snapshots, including those created on your other devices, merges them with local records and stores the result on your device. File identifiers, creation times and installation tags are also read to find and prune snapshots.

After a purchase, the settings also contain a signed document confirming ad removal. It travels with the settings in Google Drive snapshots and JSON exports. The document is not technically bound to a Google account: its signature verifies the document's authenticity, not account ownership. Transfer is intended for your own devices; do not publish or give others a full backup containing this document. It contains no card details or email from the payment form.

When exchanges occur. The first synchronization runs after connection. Automatic synchronization is enabled by default; you can turn it off on the cloud synchronization screen. When enabled, synchronization also runs after changes, at app launch and in the background when a network is available. Manual synchronization is available too. Turning off automatic mode does not delete existing copies.

Recipients. Authorization requests and snapshots go directly from your device to Google services. Your other devices receive snapshots when connected to the same account. The developer has no administrative access to your Drive. Google data is not supplied to the advertising SDK. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Export and restoration

“Save to file” creates a JSON file in a location you choose. Restoration reads only the file you select. The backup contains diary records, settings, change metadata and, after a purchase, the signed ad-removal document, but not your Google email, account identifier or tokens. The app does not encrypt this JSON file. If you choose a cloud file provider or send a backup to someone, you select the recipient, whose rules apply separately.

4. Advertising and technical data

The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK and its AppMetrica and Varioqub dependencies. Loading and displaying ads requires connections to advertising services. Yandex may process your IP address, technical request, device and software information, available advertising and technical identifiers, diagnostics, and ad impression and interaction information. Components and their purposes are listed in the SDK and service list.

How advertising works. While verified ad removal is not active, the advertising SDK may start when the app or ad placements open, without a separate prior consent dialog. The app sends a flag indicating that consent has not been given and disables location tracking and App Ad Analytics reporting. These settings do not disable the SDK or mean there is no network exchange. There is no separate advertising-consent switch. In versions where purchasing is available, verification of a signed license makes the app hide ads, stop its own ad requests and release cached advertising objects. This does not delete data previously received by the provider or guarantee that every background operation of an already running third-party SDK stops immediately.

Precise and approximate location permissions are not requested. The Android build includes the advertising identifier permission AD_ID; its availability depends on the device and system. Where supported, you can manage the advertising identifier in Android settings; this does not stop all technical data processing.

The app does not add food records, weight, measurements, notes, goals, Google email or Google account identifier to ad request parameters or use them for ad targeting. Watching an ad is not a condition for saving records. The offline diary works without a network, but disconnecting the network also prevents Google synchronization.

Processing, retention and deletion requests for advertising service data are governed by the Yandex Privacy Policy. The diary developer does not set a separate retention period for that data and cannot promise that clearing the diary deletes it.

6. Security, permissions and processing locations

Google exchanges use HTTPS. Sign-in uses the system browser, request-state validation and PKCE. The local Google session, including tokens, is encrypted with AES-GCM using an Android Keystore key. Disconnecting the account deletes its local session file and connection cache. Yandex states that SDK data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS.

The local diary and settings are protected by Android app isolation. The app does not separately encrypt the diary, exported JSON or cloud JSON snapshots; there is no end-to-end encryption between devices. System backup of app data is disabled. Migration from an older format may leave an internal backup.

The app uses internet and network-state access. Background work components also declare Android technical permissions for wake locks, rescheduling after device boot and foreground services. Import and export files are chosen through the system picker; broad access to all storage is not requested. Camera, microphone, contacts, SMS and location permissions are not requested.

Diagnostic logs written by the app's own code contain result codes without diary contents, email, tokens or network response bodies; these logs are not separately sent to the developer. This statement does not exclude technical diagnostics by the third-party advertising SDK.

The payment server and payment form use HTTPS. The license document's signature verifies its authenticity, but does not encrypt the document or bind it to a person's identity.

The operator is located in Russia. Google, Yandex, GitHub, Robokassa and Cloudflare process data under their respective policies; external infrastructure may be located in other countries. In particular, the payment database is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure rather than on your device. The app does not offer a storage-region selector or promise that all data is stored in Russia or mainland China. Availability of third-party services depends on the region and network; the main offline diary features do not require them.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records are retained until you change or delete them or clear app data. Deleting an individual record hides it from the interface and sends a deletion marker during synchronization. Merge metadata may retain earlier values of deleted diary fields so that old snapshots do not restore the record. Earlier values may also remain in old cloud snapshots and exported backups.

When data changes, a new full snapshot is created in Google Drive. After a successful upload, the app attempts to delete older snapshots from the same installation, retaining the new snapshot and one previous snapshot. Snapshots from other installations are not affected; failed cleanup can leave extra copies. There is no fixed automatic deletion period for all snapshots.

The Google session is retained until you disconnect it or clear app storage; an invalid token may require another sign-in. “Disconnect account” stops synchronization on this device and deletes the local session, but keeps the diary and cloud copies, does not revoke the Google permission itself and does not delete your Google account.

The license document remains in your settings and the backups you create until you delete them. Clearing every copy may leave the app without the document needed to restore ad removal. Order records are retained while needed to confirm and restore the license, account for transactions, handle requests and meet applicable requirements. There is no single fixed period for all these purposes; contact support about retention and deletion. Clearing the diary does not delete the server's order record, mandatory transaction records held by Robokassa and the seller, or previously issued payment documents. Refund requests are handled under the seller's offer (in Russian).

To delete all copies:

  1. Disconnect Google on all devices. If you cannot access the app, first revoke its access in Google Account connections.
  2. Open Google Drive settings for the relevant account, choose “Manage apps”, find the app under the name shown at sign-in, then choose “Options” → “Delete hidden app data”. Use desktop site mode or a computer if necessary.
  3. Revoke Google access if you have not already done so. Revoking access alone does not delete snapshots.
  4. Clear app data in Android settings or uninstall the app on every device. This also removes internal backups and the local session.
  5. Separately delete exported JSON files from your chosen folders and cloud storage.

Reconnecting a device that still holds records can create a cloud copy again. Detailed instructions are available without reinstalling the app. The developer cannot remotely erase your devices or personal Drive; do not send passwords or tokens.
